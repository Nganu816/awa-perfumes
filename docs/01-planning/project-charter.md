## 2. Project Purpose & Justification

### 2.1 Business Need
AWA Perfumes requires a modern, scalable e-commerce platform to:
- Expand global market reach
- Provide premium digital shopping experience
- Enable direct-to-consumer sales
- Build brand loyalty through digital channels

### 2.2 Business Case
- Current market: $50B global perfume e-commerce market
- Target: Capture 0.01% market share = $5M annual revenue
- ROI: 300% within 24 months

## 3. Project Objectives

### 3.1 SMART Goals
| Goal | Metric | Timeline |
|------|--------|----------|
| Launch platform | Go-live | 8 weeks |
| Achieve 10K monthly visitors | Analytics | Month 3 |
| Process 500 orders/month | Sales data | Month 6 |
| 99.9% uptime | Monitoring | Ongoing |
| WCAG 2.1 AA compliance | Audit | Launch |

## 4. Scope

### 4.1 In Scope
- Customer-facing e-commerce website
- Product catalog (perfumes, gift sets, accessories)
- Shopping cart and checkout
- Payment integration (Stripe, PayPal)
- User accounts and authentication
- Order management
- Legal pages (Returns, T&C, Privacy, NPA 2023, Security)
- Admin dashboard (basic)
- Email notifications

### 4.2 Out of Scope
- Mobile native apps (Phase 2)
- Multi-vendor marketplace
- Physical POS integration
- Warehouse management system
- Advanced AI recommendations (Phase 2)

## 7. Assumptions

1. Development team has Next.js and Python expertise
2. Product photography and content will be provided
3. Domain name already registered
4. Business legal review of all policy documents
5. Stripe and PayPal merchant accounts approved

## 8. Constraints

1. Launch within 8 weeks
2. Budget not to exceed $50,000
3. Must comply with NPA 2023, GDPR, PCI DSS
4. Must achieve WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility
5. Core Web Vitals scores must be "Good"

| Security vulnerabilities | Medium | High | Security audit, pen testing |
| Performance issues | Low | Medium | Load testing, CDN |
| Compliance gaps | Low | High | Legal review, DPO consultation |

# Business Case: AWA Perfumes E-Commerce

## Executive Summary

AWA Perfumes aims to establish a premium online presence to capture the growing digital fragrance market. This project will deliver a state-of-the-art e-commerce platform positioned to generate $5M in annual revenue within 24 months.

## Market Analysis

### Industry Overview
- Global perfume market: $50B (2026)
- E-commerce segment: 35% growth YoY
- Online fragrance sales: $17.5B

### Target Market
| Segment | Demographics | Size |
|---------|--------------|------|
| Primary | Women 25-45, urban, high income | 40% |
| Secondary | Men 25-50, professionals | 35% |
| Tertiary | Gift buyers, all demographics | 25% |

### Competitive Analysis
| Competitor | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|------------|-----------|------------|
| Sephora | Brand recognition, samples | Generic experience |
| FragranceX | Discount pricing | Low brand perception |
| Brand sites | Premium experience | Limited selection |

### AWA Differentiation
- Curated luxury experience
- Personalized scent profiles
- Niche/artisan brands
- Exceptional customer service
